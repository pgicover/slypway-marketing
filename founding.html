What lifetime means

Lifetime here means lifetime of the Slypway plan you choose, as it exists once we reach general availability. It does not mean lifetime of you personally, and it does not automatically include any future stand-alone product we sell outside that plan.

A founding seat is tied to one agency, not to one person.

Arlo AI usage carries a fair-use limit: a set number of AI actions a month per seat. Beyond that, usage is billed at cost, never absorbed into your one-time price.

Card processing fees and any other pass-through costs are never included in the USD$100 or USD$499 price.

A founding seat cannot be transferred or resold to another agency.

Refund promise

Ask for a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions asked. If the plan you bought has not reached general availability within 12 months of your purchase, you can ask for a full refund then too.

How feedback works

Feedback is light and optional. We will reach out three times: at 30, 90, and 180 days after you join, each time with a short set of questions or the option of a short call. Nothing scheduled beyond that, and nothing mandatory.

Other builders have shown this works

Honest questions

What if Slypway shuts down or changes direction?

Your refund rights still apply for the windows above. Beyond that, a founding seat carries the same risk as backing anything early: we are telling you plainly what we know and do not know today.

Why is this not unlimited Arlo usage?

Every AI action has a real cost. A fair-use limit keeps the price honest instead of hiding a future price increase in the fine print.

Can I move my seat to a different agency later?

No. Founding seats are non-transferable and tied to one agency.

What exactly do I get today, since the plans are not finished?

A recorded entitlement to the tier you choose, honored once that plan reaches general availability, plus a direct feedback channel with us while we build it. You are not getting finished software today.

What happens to my seat number if I get a refund?

It stays retired. Refunded seats are never reissued or resold to anyone else.
