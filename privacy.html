Privacy policy

Our commitment

Slypway Inc. (Slypway, we, us) treats protecting personal information as a basic part of running Slypway. This policy covers any personal information we collect or hold, including information we pass to a third party to do the work you asked us to do.

What counts as personal information

Personal information is anything that identifies a person: a name, a phone number, an email address, or similar details, including information that can be matched with other data to identify someone.

Our privacy framework

Our privacy practices follow the ten principles of Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). We collect personal information to verify who you are, provide Slypway to you, prevent fraud, keep your account running, and connect to third-party platforms you ask us to connect to. We share it internally only to deliver the service, and with outside parties only where needed to complete something you asked for, using the minimum needed each time. When you visit Slypway we record basic visit information (the page, the referring site, your approximate location, your device, and your IP address) to understand interest in Slypway.

1. Consent

We collect and use your personal information only with your consent, given directly or through your use of Slypway.

2. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time, except where a law or regulation requires otherwise. Withdrawing consent may limit what parts of Slypway you can keep using.

3. When we act without consent

We only collect information without consent where there is an emergency affecting someone's life, a legal requirement to do so, or a fraud investigation.

4. Protecting your data

Employees who can access your data are bound to keep it confidential, and we use security measures intended to prevent unauthorized access.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as it is needed to provide Slypway or as long as the law requires. [Gap: a specific retention period, in months or years, has not been confirmed and is not stated here.]

6. Checking your information

You can ask to review the personal information we hold about you, subject to privacy and proprietary limits.

7. Correcting it

If something we hold about you is wrong, tell us and we will correct what we can. Where the information came from a third party, we will point you to them.

8. Who we share it with

You can ask us which organizations have received your personal information.

9. Responding to you

We respond promptly to requests about your personal information.

10. Third-party information

Files that contain your organization's confidential information, or our own proprietary data, stay confidential. Factual information directly about you is available to you on request.
